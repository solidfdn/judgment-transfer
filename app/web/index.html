    Review the instructions from six perspectives
    Use these questions with the task owner when reviewing the draft and the results.
    1. Purpose and scope: What needs doing, and where does the responsibility end?
    2. Required information: What must be known, and where can it be checked?
    3. Steps and operations: What is done, in what order, and how?
    4. Conditions and reasons: When does the action change, and why?
    5. Exceptions and human review: When should work stop, and what must a person confirm?
    6. Completion: What counts as finished, and how is the result checked?
    These are review questions, not six automatically calculated scores.
