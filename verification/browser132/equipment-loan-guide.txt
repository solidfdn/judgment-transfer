Cedarline Services — equipment loan desk guide
Purpose: review an equipment loan request and decide whether it can join the collection queue or needs the desk manager's decision.
Work only in the isolated review record. Do not reserve or release equipment, contact anyone, or change an inventory system.
The request records borrower type as EMPLOYEE or CONTRACTOR, requested loan days as a whole number, and two checkboxes: equipment available and sponsor confirmed.
Copy the two checkboxes as recorded. They are facts from the request, not judgments made by the reviewer.
Every new request begins with review status NEW.
An EMPLOYEE request may be marked READY_FOR_COLLECTION when requested loan days are from 1 to 5 inclusive, equipment available is Yes, and sponsor confirmed is Yes.
A request for more than 5 days requires the desk manager's decision and must remain HELD, even when equipment is available and the sponsor has confirmed.
If a required value is missing, a checkbox is No, the borrower type is unknown, or requested loan days are less than 1, keep the request HELD and ask for the specific missing information or decision.
This guide does not state whether CONTRACTOR requests can join the ordinary collection queue. Ask the equipment desk owner to explain that rule.
The review is complete when the isolated review status is READY_FOR_COLLECTION or HELD and the reason is recorded.
